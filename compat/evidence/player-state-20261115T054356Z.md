## Bug found and fixed during this verification

The first full run exposed a mirror-side corruption: after the owner selected hotbar
slot 8, the mirror had the golden sword in **both** slot 8 and slot 0. Cause:
`EntitySyncManager.applyEquipment` applied the owner's `ClientboundSetEquipmentPacket`
with `Entity.setItemSlot(MAINHAND, ...)`, and `Player.setItemSlot` writes
`Inventory.items[selected]`. The entity tracker sends the equipment packet earlier in
the tick than `PlayerStateSyncManager` sends `PlayerSelectedSlotPacket`, so the sword
landed in the mirror's still-selected slot 0. `applyEquipment` now keeps the mirror's
own main hand for `ExternalPlayer` (the inventory channel carries the stack) and only
applies absolute slots (armor, off hand) from the packet. Re-running the full check
gave 21/21 with no stray stack.
